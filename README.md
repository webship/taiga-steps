# @webship-js/taiga-steps

Cucumber step definitions for Taiga, the open-source agile project management platform, a pack for
[webship-js](https://github.com/webship/webship-js) (Playwright and Cucumber): logging in, a project and who may open
it, its backlog, its sprints and its board, one user story, its comments, status, assignee and tags, a new user story,
and what a member with a lesser role may not do.

The steps, their documentation and CI come with the first release, `2.0.0`.
